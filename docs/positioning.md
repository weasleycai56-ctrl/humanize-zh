`humanize-zh`

可解释的中文 AI 文风检查与分级改写 Skill；检查写作模式，不判断作者身份。

## Short description — English

Explainable Chinese writing-style linter and humanizer skill—not an AI authorship detector.

## One-line pitch

让中文少一点套话、宣传腔和机械结构，多一点具体、清楚与可信。

## About paragraph — bilingual

Humanize ZH 面向中文写作习惯，诊断空泛开场、意义膨胀、宣传腔、机械排比、模糊归因、格式滥用与强行总结，并按 casual、professional、academic、social、marketing 场景进行分级改写。它识别的是可观察的文风模式，不判断文本由谁创作。

Humanize ZH is an explainable Chinese writing-style linter and rewriting skill. It diagnoses observable style patterns across casual, professional, academic, social, and marketing writing. It does not detect AI authorship.

## Differentiation

- Chinese-first taxonomy with legitimate-use boundaries, not a translated English blacklist.
- Explainable diagnostics before rewriting.
- Facts, citations, uncertainty, and regional language are explicit invariants.
- Deterministic local audit plus agent-guided contextual rewriting.
- Behavioral fixtures designed for maintainers, including warnings against invented specificity.

## Claims to avoid

- “detects AI-generated text” or any accuracy percentage;
- “guaranteed to pass AI detectors”;
- “the first,” “the best,” or “industry-leading” without independent evidence;
- stars, downloads, users, testimonials, partners, or contributors that are not publicly verifiable.
